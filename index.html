Ph.D. Candidate, Agricultural & Resource Economics

I am an applied economist working at the intersection of environmental economics, international trade, and agricultural economics. My research examines how trade shapes the environmental footprint of our food system. I combine causal inference methods and economic modeling with remotely sensed data and machine learning to study these changes at scale.

I am on the job market in the 2026–2027 academic year and can be reached at paobrien@ucdavis.edu.

Research

Job Market Paper

Feeding the Export Boom: Technological Change and Land Use in Brazil's Beef Industry

Latest version (PDF)

Abstract. This paper studies whether increased export market exposure induces agricultural technological change and how that response affects land use. I examine Brazil's beef export boom to China, exploiting staggered plant authorizations combined with a novel panel of feedlot development that I construct using satellite imagery and deep learning. I first document that beef-processing plants increasingly source younger cattle and draw more heavily from regions with feedlots after gaining access to the Chinese export market. Using complementary empirical strategies that leverage the unique structure of the China export authorization process, I find that greater exposure to the Chinese market substantially increased feedlot entry and confined-finishing capacity. My main estimate implies that municipalities with a one-standard-deviation increase in exposure build 3.8 times as many feedlots over the sample period. Holding current beef production fixed, my back-of-the-envelope estimates suggest that replacing this China-induced feedlot finishing with pasture finishing would require approximately 1.29 million additional hectares of pasture, which exceeds the observed total expansion of Brazil's pasture area between 2004 and 2024, and would imply 169 MtCO2 of potential carbon stock losses. The results show how trade can affect environmental resource use by inducing changes in production technology.
